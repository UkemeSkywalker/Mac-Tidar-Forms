# Google Stitch prompts: Mac Tidar Forms

Start each Stitch session with the shared style, then paste one screen prompt. Attach the three reference images as well if Stitch allows it. Export results to `designs/<screen>/`.

## Shared style

Mac Tidar Forms is a form builder from Mac Tidar, used by small African businesses and teams. Match the attached reference posters: a clean digital-marketing carousel, airy and mostly white, with one strong portrait and one yellow highlight.

**Background.** Near-white #FBFBFB with a graph-paper grid of thin 1px light grey #E1E1E1 lines. Squares are even, about 44px on a 736px-wide layout (6% of the width), and run edge to edge behind everything. Leave plenty of it empty. No cards or panels on marketing pages: text and images sit directly on the grid.

**Text.** Montserrat throughout. Near-black #0A0A0A, left-aligned, sentence or title case, never all caps.
- Headline: Bold to ExtraBold, tight line spacing, one to two short lines, about 6.5% of the layout width in size. Confident, not oversized.
- Subline: Light or Regular, same family, about 4% of the width, placed right under the headline. It can be pushed to the right so it steps away from the headline.
- Some sublines finish with a few Bold words, and those words carry the highlight.
- Small labels such as "Share it" are Bold with a small solid curved arrow beside them, and sit in the bottom corner.

**Colour.** About 60% near-white, then black and greys. Yellow #FAE41B is the only brand accent and stays sparing, about 1 to 3% of any screen. Small realistic accents can appear inside illustrations only: metallic gold, pale blue burst ticks #A9CBE8, short red arcs #E5484D and a pink glow. Text is never coloured except by the highlight.

**Highlight.** One name or key phrase per headline sits on a yellow dry-marker swipe: rough, uneven edges, streaky at the ends, tilted a degree or two, extending slightly past the word or stopping short of it. Black text stays on top. Never a clean rectangle.

**Lines.** All hand-drawn:
- Corner scribble: a loose diagonal crosshatch mesh of round yellow marker strokes, 4 to 5px thick, about 8 to 10 strokes, cropped by the top-right corner. Only one per screen.
- Arrow: a single near-black pen stroke that curves and tapers, with a small open head, pointing at the person or key element.
- Burst ticks: three to five short tapered strokes radiating around a prop.
- No geometric dividers or thick borders anywhere.

**People.** Fictional African professionals, never a real or recognisable person. One large head-and-shoulders or waist-up figure, anchored bottom-right, taking up about half the layout height, with the body running off the bottom edge. Use one treatment per screen:
- Low-poly vector portrait made of visible triangular facets, either natural skin tones or fully greyscale, with a dark jacket and small sparkle highlights.
- High-contrast black-and-white photo cutout with a thick, slightly uneven yellow sticker outline following the silhouette.

**Props.** One symbolic collage object beside the person, often a black-and-white cutout hand holding something in colour. For this product, use a glass jar full of paper form slips with yellow ticks, or floating spreadsheet cells.

**App controls.** Keep the same type, grid and yellow. Corners 8px, thin #E1E1E1 borders on white, no drop shadows, gradients or thick black outlines. Primary buttons are near-black with white text. Yellow marks the active or selected state. Responsive, usable at 375px wide. Poster styling belongs in headings and empty states, and dense data stays plain.

## 1. Marketing homepage

Design the homepage, with people as the main visual. Small logo and "Mac Tidar Forms" top-left, Sign in at the top right, and the corner scribble top-right behind it.

Hero: headline "Build a form. Watch the **answers** add up." with "answers" on the yellow swipe. Subline, stepped to the right: "Every response in a spreadsheet and on a dashboard." A near-black "Create your first form" button. Bottom-right, a large low-poly portrait of a smiling African woman in a dark blazer, with sparkles. To her left, a black-and-white hand comes in from the page edge holding a glass jar of paper form slips with pale blue burst ticks and red arcs. A hand-drawn arrow curves from the headline toward her.

Then three airy sections directly on the grid, each with one headline, one highlighted word and one image, alternating sides: "**Build** it in minutes", "**Share** one link" and "**See** what people said". Use a black-and-white sticker-outline cutout of two colleagues laughing over a laptop in one section, and floating spreadsheet cells in another. Close with a large headline and the same button, with a bold "Share it" and small curved arrow bottom-left.

## 2. Sign in and sign up

A split screen on the grid. Left: a white panel with thin border. Sign in has email, password, a near-black button and a link to create an account. Sign up has name, email and password with an 8-character hint. Include an inline error state in red. Right, hidden on phones: headline "Good **questions** get good answers." and a greyscale low-poly portrait anchored bottom-right.

## 3. Forms list

The signed-in home on the grid. Header with logo, user name and Sign out. Headline "Your **forms**" with a near-black New form button. A responsive grid of white cards with thin borders; each has a form title, a yellow Live pill or grey Draft pill, a response count and the last-updated date. Empty state: headline "Your first form is **one click** away", a hand-drawn arrow toward New form, and a portrait bottom-right.

## 4. Form builder

A full-screen, desktop-first drag-and-drop form editor in the shared style. Use the attached form builder image for layout only: its structure, not its colours, shadows or fonts.

**Far-left rail:** a narrow white icon rail with logo, Forms, Templates, Responses, Dashboard and sign out at the bottom. The active icon sits on a yellow marker swipe.

**Top bar:** thin white bar. Left: back arrow, editable form title and the form's link in small grey text. Centre: icon tools for Questions, Pages, Theme, Logic and Settings, with Undo and Redo. Right: Preview, Share, a near-black Publish button and the user's avatar and name.

**Left panel:** a white panel with two tabs, Blocks and Templates, and a search box. The active tab has a small yellow swipe.
- **Blocks tab:** a two-column grid of draggable tiles, each with a simple line icon and label, grouped under small headings.
  - Questions: short answer, paragraph, email, number, date, multiple choice, checkboxes, dropdown, rating.
  - Media: picture, video.
  - Layout and text: heading, text, divider, columns, section.
  - Elements: hand-drawn arrow, highlight swipe, scribble, sticker.
- **Templates tab:** a scrollable list of predesigned forms with small thumbnails, filter chips by solution, and a Use template action. Show customer feedback, event registration, job application, contact, order form, survey, booking and client intake.

**Canvas, on the graph-paper background:** a device toggle for desktop, tablet and phone, with zoom controls above the canvas. The form appears as its respondent will see it, with a title, a picture banner, a video block, a hand-drawn arrow pointing at a question, and several question blocks.

**Show drag and drop in action:**
- A tile being dragged from the panel, with a tilted ghost following the cursor and a yellow insertion line on the canvas where it will land.
- One selected block with a dashed near-black outline, a small yellow label tab naming the block, a drag handle, and duplicate and delete actions.
- A small settings popover on the selected block for required, help text and options, or for upload and replace on media blocks. Arrows and stickers show resize and rotate handles.

Also show the empty canvas: "**Drag** your first block here", with a hand-drawn arrow from the left panel and a link to start from a template. No portrait on this screen.

## 5. Public form

The page a respondent sees, with no app navigation, on the grid. A narrow column: bold form title and light description directly on the grid, then one white card per question, showing every question type. Rating is a row of five square buttons that fill yellow when selected. Required questions have a red asterisk. Include a validation error state, a near-black Submit button, a small "Powered by Mac Tidar Forms" footer, and a thank-you state with "**Thank** you." on a yellow swipe, a corner scribble and a sticker-outline cutout anchored bottom-right. Must feel excellent on a phone.

## 6. Responses spreadsheet

A spreadsheet view on the Spreadsheet tab: search box, "4 of 4" count, a near-black Download Excel button and a white Download CSV button. A dense white grid with a sticky pale grey header in bold, row numbers, thin #E1E1E1 cell borders, horizontal scroll and a quiet Delete on each row. Keep the data plain and readable. Empty state: "**No answers** yet" with a light subline "Share your link to get started" and a hand-drawn arrow.

## 7. Dashboard

The Dashboard tab: three plain stat tiles with large bold numbers (Total responses, Last 7 days, Latest response), a thin-column chart of responses per day for 30 days in near-black, then a two-column grid of question cards. Choice questions use thin horizontal near-black bars on pale grey with count and percentage, rating adds an average, number shows average, min and max, and text shows the latest five answers. Include hover tooltips and an empty state.
