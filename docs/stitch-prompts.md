# Google Stitch prompts: Mac Tidar Forms

Start each Stitch session with the shared style, then paste one screen prompt. Export results to `designs/<screen>/`.

## Shared style

Mac Tidar Forms is a form builder from Mac Tidar, used by small African businesses and teams. The look is a bold marketing poster drawn on graph paper, in the spirit of a punchy social media carousel.

- **Background:** white #FFFFFF with a thin, pale grey graph-paper grid (#E9E9E6, 40px squares) across the page.
- **Type:** Montserrat. Headlines are extra bold, tight, near-black #111111 and very large. Secondary lines are light weight and sit directly under the headline.
- **Signature highlight:** one or two key words in each headline sit on a rough yellow #FFE600 highlighter swipe with uneven, dry-brush edges.
- **Hand-drawn accents:** yellow scribbled cross-hatching tucked into a page corner, and thick near-black hand-drawn curved arrows that point at the important thing.
- **Colour discipline:** only black, white, greys and yellow. Red #D92D20 is used only for errors.
- **People:** real-looking fictional African professionals, cut out of their background like a paper collage, in high-contrast black and white, with a thick yellow sticker outline around the silhouette. Warm, confident expressions. Never use a real or recognisable person.
- **Components:** square-ish 8px corners, 2px near-black borders, flat fills, hard offset shadows (4px, near-black) on primary cards. Primary buttons are yellow with black text and a black border. No gradients or soft shadows.
- **Layout:** responsive web app, usable at 375px wide. App screens stay calm and legible: the poster energy goes in headings and empty states, not in dense data.

## 1. Marketing homepage

Design the homepage, with people as the main visual. Top bar: logo and Sign in. Hero: huge headline "Build a form. Watch the **answers** add up." with "answers" on a yellow swipe, and a light subline "Then see every response in a spreadsheet and on a dashboard." Yellow button "Create your first form". On the right, a large black-and-white cutout of a smiling African woman with a coffee mug at her laptop, with a yellow sticker outline, a hand-drawn arrow curving from the headline toward her, and a small tilted paper card beside her showing a form response. Yellow scribble in the top-right corner.

Then three poster-style sections, each with a bold headline, a yellow-swipe keyword and a person or product collage: "**Build** it in minutes", "**Share** one link" and "**See** what people said". Include a section with a black-and-white cutout of two colleagues laughing over a laptop, and a closing band with a large headline and the same yellow button.

## 2. Sign in and sign up

A split screen on graph paper. Left: a bordered white card. Sign in has email, password, a yellow button and a link to create an account. Sign up has name, email and password with an 8-character hint. Include an inline error state. Right, hidden on phones: a black-and-white person cutout with a yellow sticker outline and the headline "Good **questions** get good answers."

## 3. Forms list

The signed-in home. Header with logo, user name and Sign out. Headline "Your **forms**" with a yellow New form button. A responsive grid of white cards with 2px black borders; each has a form title, a Live pill (yellow) or Draft pill (white), a response count and the last-updated date. Empty state: a person cutout, a hand-drawn arrow pointing at New form, and the line "Your first form is **one click** away."

## 4. Form builder

The question editor. Tabs: Questions, Spreadsheet, Dashboard, where the active tab has a yellow swipe behind it. A sticky toolbar with Save, Publish or Unpublish, Copy link, Preview and a quiet Delete form. A centred column with a title and description card, then one white bordered card per question: question text, type selector (short answer, paragraph, email, number, date, multiple choice, checkboxes, dropdown, rating 1 to 5), help text, editable options with remove buttons, a Required toggle, move up and down, and remove. Finish with a dashed Add question button.

## 5. Public form

The page a respondent sees, with no app navigation, on graph paper. A narrow column with a title card, which has a thick black top border and a bold title, then one white card per question, showing every question type. Rating is a row of five square buttons that fill yellow when selected. Required questions have a red asterisk. Include a validation error state, a yellow Submit button, a small "Powered by Mac Tidar Forms" footer, and a thank-you state with a person cutout and the headline "**Thank** you." on a yellow swipe. Must feel excellent on a phone.

## 6. Responses spreadsheet

A spreadsheet view on the Spreadsheet tab: search box, "4 of 4" count, a yellow Download Excel button and a white Download CSV button. A dense white grid with a sticky near-black header with white text, row numbers, thin grey cell borders, horizontal scroll and a quiet Delete on each row. Keep the data plain and readable. Empty state: a hand-drawn arrow and the line "**No answers** yet. Share your link."

## 7. Dashboard

The Dashboard tab: three stat tiles with large numbers (Total responses, Last 7 days, Latest response), a thin-column chart of responses per day for 30 days in near-black, then a two-column grid of question cards. Choice questions use horizontal bars with count and percentage, rating adds an average, number shows average, min and max, and text shows the latest five answers. Bars are near-black on pale grey. Include hover tooltips and an empty state.
